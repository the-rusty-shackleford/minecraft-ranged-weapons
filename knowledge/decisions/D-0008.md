# D-0008 — Ammunition is a family tag for what loads and an item for what the weapon's own round is

**Decision**: A profile carries two ammunition fields. `ammo_family` is an
item tag (written with its `#`): every member loads. `ammo` stays an item:
the weapon's native round, for drops, recipes and tooltips. A weapon with
a family accepts the family; without one, only its native round; with
neither, nothing. The protocol ships `small`, `medium`, `large` and
`shell` under `rangedweapons:ammo/`, empty, plus `#rangedweapons:ammo` as
their parent; any mod declares more under `<namespace>:ammo/<family>`.
At reload, an empty family and a native round outside its own family are
each reported once.

**Why**: Rusty wanted weapons tagged with an ammunition type -- his words,
an `AmmoType` enum with `MEDIUM` for the machine gun and `POTATO` for a
future launcher -- so that rounds are interchangeable across guns and
mods. Item tags are that enum, and they are open: a datapack extends them
and a mod adds a family without touching the protocol. Two fields rather
than letting `ammo` take a tag because a tag has no first member: a killed
pillager must drop a concrete item, a recipe must make one, and tag order
is not guaranteed. Small, medium and large were Rusty's spine; shell is
added because shotguns are already in the pack and a shell is a different
cartridge, not a size.

**Alternatives rejected**: *One field accepting `#tag` or an item* — see
above. *A closed enum in code* — every new family would be a protocol
release. *Per-round stats now* — nothing needs them; a data map keyed by
round item layers on later without touching this.

**Follow-ups**: The lineup Rusty has planned maps pistol to small,
rifles to medium, shotgun to shell; per-round behaviour, if it comes, is
a separate data map.
