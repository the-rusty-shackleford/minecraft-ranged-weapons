# D-0002 — The fallback tier lives inside the protocol mod

**Decision**: The protocol mod itself ships a working weapon for any item with
a profile: `ProfiledWeapon` (rounds in the `rangedweapons:rounds` component)
and `ProfiledBullet` (a gravity-free tracer dealing the profile's damage
exactly, gone after its lifetime), plus the renderer the entity type requires.
It is not a separate "rangedweapons-fallback" mod and not the consumer's job.

**Why**: Two reasons, one for users and one for tests. For users, a datapack
profile alone must make an item shootable — that is the zero-cooperation path
for closed-source gun mods, and it is worthless if it needs a second jar the
pack author has to know about. For tests, the fallback is the real backend a
consumer's tests fire when no gun mod is on the classpath: Armed Pillagers'
gametests pass on a clean clone with no third-party jar, against a real
`RangedWeapon`, not a mock. Splitting it out would make the protocol jar
"an interface with nothing behind it" and push a mock into every consumer.

`ProfiledBullet` replaces three `AbstractArrow` behaviours, each for a reason
read from the vanilla source: arrows deal `ceil(|velocity| * baseDamage)` and
lose one percent of speed per tick, so damage would arrive rounded and
diminished; arrows stick in blocks for a minute; arrows can be picked up.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Fire a vanilla arrow with no gravity* — looks and
sticks like an arrow, has no lifetime, and its damage formula is the one above.
*Leave it to the consumer* — every consumer writes the same projectile, and
the protocol's "any profiled item works" promise becomes a suggestion.
*`setBaseDamage(damage / speed)` on a plain arrow* (the first plan) —
off-by-one from float rounding at tick zero and under-dealing after drag; the
gametest that saw a golem at exactly 95.0 after one 5-damage hit is the proof
the override was needed.

**Follow-ups**: The tracer texture is generated, three texels tall; if the
protocol ever ships to a wider audience it deserves an artist's.
