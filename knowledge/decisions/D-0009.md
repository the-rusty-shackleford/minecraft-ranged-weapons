# D-0009 — Bullets bypass the hurt cooldown, push by a shared-out knockback, and a round may change what a weapon fires

**Decision**: The fallback bullet deals a damage type of the protocol's
own, `rangedweapons:bullet`, tagged `is_projectile` and
`bypasses_cooldown`. Profiles gain an optional `knockback`, the push of a
full hit in the bow's Punch units, shared equally among a shot's
projectiles; the bullet applies its share along its line of flight. A
round may carry an entry in a new data map, `rangedweapons:ammo`, naming
the numbers it changes; a store records the round it was last filled with
(`load(stack, count, ammo)`, `loadedAmmo`), and the fallback weapon's
`stats(stack)` is the profile with that entry applied.

**Why**: Rusty's friends reported a shotgun that took three blasts to kill
a sheep. The numbers were fine; the game's hurt cooldown was not: a hit
within ten ticks of another deals only its excess, so six pellets landing
together dealt one pellet's worth, and a burst at three ticks landed one
round in three. Another Gun Mod, whose numbers the players compare to,
clears the cooldown before every hit; the tag is the data-driven way to
the same end, and it belongs to the bullet, not to any gun. Knockback was
asked for "at level 3", which is a per-shot notion -- so it is shared out,
and a partial hit pushes partially. Slugs were asked for, which is a
round changing the shot: the family already says what loads; the ammo
map says what it does, and the loaded round has to be remembered because
a magazine is a count.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Clearing `invulnerableTime` before `hurt`* —
works, but poking a vanilla field the protocol does not own, invisible to
a pack. *Knockback per projectile* — six pellets would push six times.
*A second profile per round* — the weapon's own numbers (capacity, rate,
reload) are not a round's to restate.
