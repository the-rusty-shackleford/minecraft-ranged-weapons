# D-0007 — A block shot to pieces drops nothing

**Decision**: D-0005 stands in every respect but one: a block a bullet
breaks is destroyed, not mined. `destroyBlock` is called without drops. A
container still spills its contents, as it does when removed by any means.

**Why**: Rusty found himself picking up the cobblestone he had just shot a
wall into, and wants shooting a block to be a destructive act, not a way to
mine. Loot for a block is the reward for working it with the right tool;
a bullet is not a tool.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Drops with a chance* — a partial reward for a
destructive act is still a reward. *Drops for soft blocks only* — a second
list to maintain for no principle.

**Follow-ups**: none.
