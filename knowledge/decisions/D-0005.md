# D-0005 — Bullets break blocks by hardness, players by default, mobs only if the server says

**Decision**: The fallback bullet damages the block it hits. A block's
health is its vanilla hardness times a config rate (15), damage accumulates
per position for 400 ticks and shows as the vanilla crack stages, and the
block breaks with its ordinary drops when the total reaches its health.
Hardness at or above a config ceiling (20), negative hardness, and the
`#rangedweapons:bulletproof` tag are immune; `#rangedweapons:shatters`
breaks on any hit. Whose bullets may break is a common config policy:
`PLAYERS` by default, with each break passed through NeoForge's block-break
event and the level's interaction check so claim mods and spawn protection
keep their say; `EVERYONE` adds mobs under `mobGriefing`; `NOBODY` leaves
debris and sound only.

**Why**: Rusty asked that glass go instantly, tougher materials take
longer, and the toughest never go. Hardness is the one number every block
already carries that ranks materials the way players expect, so a rate and
a ceiling over it are two knobs instead of a table, and a pack retunes them
without code. The policy exists because the same bullet is fired by every
mob armed through the protocol: Armed Pillagers' raiders would otherwise
shatter every village window by default, and a player's bullet through a
neighbour's wall is what claim mods exist to stop, so the player path goes
through the event those mods listen to.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A per-block table* — a maintenance burden that
hardness already answers. *Breaking only tagged blocks* — Rusty wants the
whole material spectrum, not a list. *Mobs always* — griefing by default.
*A server config* — common is one file on both sides and needs no world.

**Follow-ups**: The `EVERYONE` path is not gametested: the policy is one
config shared by every concurrently running test.
