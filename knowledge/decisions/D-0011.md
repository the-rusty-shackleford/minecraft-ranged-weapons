# D-0011 — Bullets break only glass and lanterns; every other block cracks and stands

**Decision**: Only `#rangedweapons:shatters` breaks, on the first hit from
any weapon whatever its hardness, and drops nothing (D-0007). Every other
block keeps D-0005's health (hardness times `healthPerHardness`), but it is
now only the damage that brings the block to the last crack stage: damage
is capped there and the block never breaks. A block with no hardness shows
no cracks and stands. The tag loses ice and frosted ice and gains the
lantern and the soul lantern, plus the pack's own lanterns as optional
entries: Amendments' wall lantern and wall soul lantern (Amendments swaps a
lantern hung on a wall for its own block, so without them a wall lantern
would only crack), Bosses'Rise's ship lantern and Dusty Decorations'
nautilus lantern. D-0005's policy, its bulletproof ceiling and both tags as
datapack hooks stand.

**Why**: Rusty, 2026-10-07: "I don't want the ranged weapons mod guns to
break blocks anymore. They should still break glass and other glass items
such as lanterns, but thats it. They get too destructive otherwise", then
"I'm open to them cracking blocks, but never breaking them (besides the
glass, which should break with a single shot regardless of weapon)". The
whole-spectrum breaking D-0005 built for him took walls down in a burst, and
it took every block with no hardness (TNT, slime, honey) in one round.
Keeping the cracks keeps the feedback that a hit landed. Ice is not glass,
and "but that's it" is read literally, so it cracks with the rest.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Retuning the config* (a huge
`healthPerHardness`): every block with hardness 0 still has health 0 and
still breaks at once, and the cracks would never show. *Shattering by sound*
(blocks that sound like glass or a lantern): glass's sound is also the
beacon's and packed ice's, and a beacon shot to pieces would drop nothing,
its nether star lost. *A switch between the old rule and this one*: nobody
asked for the old rule back; a server that wants walls down has the rocket
launcher, whose blast still breaks blocks (its own config).

**Verified**: GameTests `aLanternShattersOnOneRoundAndDropsNothing`,
`stoneCracksButStandsThroughEightRounds`, `aBlockWithNoHardnessStands` and
`iceIsNotGlassAndStands`, each of which failed on 1.7.0's rule (the lantern
stood; stone, slime and ice were destroyed); glass, obsidian and a mob's
round unchanged.
