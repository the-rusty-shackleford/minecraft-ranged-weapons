# D-0006 — Guns opt out of Hold My Items by writing its config, until it takes a tag

**Decision**: The protocol's client carries `HoldMyItems.excludeItems`: if
Hold My Items (`holdmyitemsnf`) is loaded, the given items' ids are merged
into its `excludedItemIds` config list through its own `ConfigValue`, found
by reflection, and saved; a gun mod calls it once with its guns. In
parallel, a pull request offers that mod an item tag to check beside its
lists, after which this hook is deleted.

**Why**: That mod replaces first-person rendering of every held item with
its own arm pose and then applies the model's first-person transform on
top, so a two-handed weapon cannot be right under both frames; its own
default exclusions are gun mods, and the only opt-out it offers is two
config lists read live every frame. Rusty did not want every new handheld
to mean a hand edit of another mod's config in every player's instance. A
mod that knows it ships guns can do that edit itself, once, for everyone.
Per item rather than per namespace so ammunition keeps the nicer arm.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Design the model to survive the pose* — the
pose is wrong for a rifle whatever the orientation. *Our own first-person
renderer for guns* — its mixin runs at the head of the vanilla method, before
any event, so this becomes a priority contest between mixins plus a copy of
vanilla's arm code. *A patched Hold My Items jar in the pack* — a fork to
carry forward on every update of theirs.

**Follow-ups**: Delete the hook once the upstream tag lands and the pack
carries that release.
